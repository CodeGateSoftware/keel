## Source 15 — "Gartley Pattern (from the 1935 original)"

> **Enriches the deferred harmonics record (§9.4)** with the *authoritative, complete* Gartley rules,
> sourced to the original: **H.M. Gartley, _Profits in the Stock Market_ (1935), the "Gartley 222"**
> (page 222, fig. 27). Scott Carney later added the Fib ratios; Larry Pesavento's _Trade What You See_
> is cited for the psychology. **Still deferred to v2** (harmonic family, overfit risk) — but now fully
> specified and provenance-backed so v2 doesn't re-derive it.

### 15.1 Behavioral essence (why the pattern exists)
Gartley described "one of the best trading opportunities" as the moment where **effort stops producing
results, volume dries up, the crowd runs out of energy, price retraces just enough to restore
confidence, and risk can be defined with surgical precision.** It's a *behavioral sequence*, not a
shape — consistent with our patterns-not-prediction thesis (§1.1). Same low-risk-entry logic as the
CTS "deceleration + rejection at a level" idea (§1.4, §8.1).

### 15.2 Full Gartley rules (authoritative) → future `strategy/rules/`, supersedes §9.4's Gartley note
Legs X → A → B → C → D (bearish version as demoed; **long mirror = entry for us**, short = exit/filter):
- **Anchor/impulse leg**, then the **X→A** leg.
- **B leg = 0.618 retracement of X–A** — must **touch 0.618** but must **NOT reach 0.786**
  (past 0.786 → it's a **Butterfly**, a different pattern).
- **C leg = 0.618 retracement of A–B**, and must **not violate A** (stay beyond A, i.e. higher than A
  in the bullish case).
- **D completion = 1.272 extension of A–B** (presenter's preferred; conventional alt = 0.786 of X–A —
  the two often coincide).
- **Deep vs shallow:** the closer B pushes toward 0.786, the **higher/closer-to-X** the D completion →
  **tighter stop → better R:R**. Prefer deep Gartleys.
- **Stop = "1.13 inversion" beyond X** (Tier One's method) + a couple ticks front-running.
- **Target = 0.382 retracement of A–D**; **roll the target with price** if it extends. Optional T2 = 0.618.
- Demoed R:R ≈ **2.5:1** (deep pattern). Entry via **limit order** at D.

### 15.3 Reinforced — the *primary* setup he actually wanted (confluence, already captured)
The Gartley was his *fallback* entry after his main setup's rules weren't met. The main setup stacked:
**double-top at a weekly resistance level + RSI overbought + RSI divergence + deceleration + rejection
candle** — a textbook CTS confluence stack (§8.1, §4.4, §1.4). The "rules weren't met, so no trade"
(the second top didn't reach the first) re-confirms the **binary, no-"close-enough"** discipline (§2.2).
