### 52.2 ⛔ New-but-excluded instruments: ETNs, ETCs, and Mutual Funds
The genuinely new material vs §51 is all **out of scope by mandate**:
- **ETNs (Exchange-Traded Notes)** — defined in the book as **"unsecured *debt* securities"** → a debt
  instrument = **riba; excluded.** Clean concrete example that a debt-based tracker is out.
- **ETCs (Exchange-Traded Commodities)** — commodity-tracking wrappers; not spot ownership of the asset and
  not our market. Out of scope (same reasoning as ETFs — a wrapper, not the coin).
- **Mutual funds** — pooled, professionally-managed fund vehicles priced at **NAV**, charging management
  fees, sometimes with **5–8yr lock-ups**. **Not our instrument** (we trade spot crypto directly, self-custodied,
  instantly liquid, no manager, no lock-up). Sub-types compound the exclusion: **money-market & fixed-income
  (bond) funds = riba** (interest/debt); **balanced funds** = the stocks/bonds asset-allocation paradigm already
  declined ([§51.3](./source-51.md)); **stock funds** = an equity wrapper. **No `CompliancePolicy` change** —
  these are simply instruments outside the spot-crypto mandate.

### 52.3 Minor reinforcements (nothing new to build)
- **Return = "distribution vs capital gains":** funds pay **distributions (dividends/interest)** *or* deliver
  **capital gains**. keel earns **only via capital gains** (buy-low/sell-high on owned spot); it never takes a
  **distribution/yield**, which for crypto is staking/lending = **riba-yield (excluded)** — reinforces the
  riba-yield / `haram_sector` screen ([§28.4](./source-28.md), [§41.1](./source-41.md)). Consistent, not new.
- **Value vs Growth (large/mid/small) stock-fund matrix** → **equity fundamental classification, N/A to crypto**
  (tokens have no intrinsic-value/earnings-growth categorization) — same finding as [§50](./source-50.md).
- **Mutual-fund lock-ups as a disadvantage** faintly reinforces our preference for **liquid, instantly-exitable
  spot positions** (capital-preservation, [§22](./source-22.md)/[§51.2](./source-51.md)). Trivial.
