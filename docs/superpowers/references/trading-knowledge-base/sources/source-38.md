### 38.1 Fundamentals "expectations / priced-in" note → reinforces no-oracle + event insight (§6.4/§34.6)
The one transferable idea from the stock-fundamentals chapter: *"a company can post **good earnings yet the
stock falls** — because of **expectations**. If good earnings were already expected, the stock is fully
valued… fundamentals can keep a stock over/undervalued far longer than you'd think."* → reinforces (a)
**no-prediction-oracle (§6.4)** — don't trade on the fundamental "should"; and (b) the **expectations /
already-priced-in** insight from the T3 earnings framework (§34.6): the reaction, not the number, is what
matters. Stanzione's own line — *"stick to a trading system and don't guess"* — restates our thesis.
**Reinforcement only, nothing to build.**
