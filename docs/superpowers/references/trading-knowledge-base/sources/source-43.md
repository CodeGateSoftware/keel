**⛔ Out of scope / excluded — options are non-permissible for a halal spot-only agent.** Buying a call is a
**leveraged bet on a derivative right, not spot ownership** → **gharar** (derivative/future agreement,
§28.1), **not-spot / no ownership** (§28.2), and the OTM-premium payoff shades into **maisir** (§27.4). This
adds nothing beyond §42's blanket options exclusion — it's the *how-to* for one excluded strategy.

- **No portable content:** the mechanics (options chain, strike $260, weekly expiries, "1 call = 100
  shares", ask $7.45) are all options-platform specifics for an instrument we never trade.
- **No new rule/rail/allowlist.** Reaffirms the standing options exclusion (§27.4 / §28.1 / §34.7 / §42.1).
- **Note:** "1 call = 100 shares" is a leverage reminder — reinforces *why* options are excluded (leverage
  + non-ownership), nothing more.

**Discarded:** the entire Swissquote-platform call-buying tutorial, author bio, marketing.
