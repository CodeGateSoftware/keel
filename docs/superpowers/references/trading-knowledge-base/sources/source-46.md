## Source 46 — "Selling a Covered Call to Open" (Swissquote options series, part 5)

> Part 5 of the Swissquote **options** series ([42](./source-42.md)–[45](./source-45.md)). Covers the
> **covered call** (own the underlying + write a call against it for premium income). **Same excluded
> instrument class — logged compactly, no extraction.**

**⛔ Out of scope / excluded — via the written-call leg.** A covered call = **long spot (fine) + a SHORT
CALL OPTION (excluded).** The spot ownership is permissible, but the written call is still an **option**
(gharar / future contract, §28.1) and collects **premium to cap your own upside** — an options-derivative
income stream, not a spot gain. So the *combined strategy* is out of scope even though its stock leg isn't.
- **Compliance note:** this is the "options income" pattern already excluded when Adam Mesh's cash-secured-
  puts / covered-call income strategies were flagged (§34.7). A halal account may *hold* the spot coin; it
  may **not write calls against it.** (The nearest permissible "income while holding" would be spot
  dividends/staking — but staking-yield is itself riba-excluded, §28.4 — so for us the answer is simply:
  hold spot, no option overlay.)
- **No new rule/rail/allowlist. No action** — reaffirms §27.4 / §28.1 / §34.7 / §42.1.
