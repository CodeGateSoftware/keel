## Source 40 — "Trading Cryptocurrencies 2: Chainlink, Tezos and more" (Swissquote primer, 13pp)

> Part 2 of the Swissquote series (companion to [Source 39](./source-39.md)). **Even thinner and further
> from strategy than Part 1** — it's a **catalog of altcoin/token profiles**: Chainlink, EOS, Stellar,
> Tezos, Ethereum Classic, Augur, 0x — describing each project's technology/purpose, then Swissquote
> account-opening marketing.
>
> **Saturation-honest: nothing actionable.** No trading strategy, indicators, risk management, or
> mechanical content of any kind — just descriptive per-project education. Logged to record it was seen.

---

### 40.1 Altcoin profiles → reinforces the allowlist-curation stance (no action)
The whole booklet is "what is Chainlink / Tezos / Stellar / EOS / ETC / Augur / 0x." → The only bearing on
our system is **allowlist curation**: these are **speculative altcoins outside our liquid-majors allowlist**
(BTC/ETH/PAXG). Our standing stance (§36 avoid-alts/memes, §28.3/§33 favor real-utility, §28.4 `haram_sector`
screen) means any such token would face the **CompliancePolicy admission gate** (halal-sector + liquidity +
5yr-data + backtestable) before it could ever enter the allowlist. **The primer supplies profiles, not the
screening data — nothing actionable.** (Note: some — e.g. **Augur**, a decentralized *prediction/betting*
market — would likely fail the **`haram_sector`/maisir** screen outright; a concrete example of why the
sector screen exists.)
- Marginal: the **Ethereum/Ethereum-Classic DAO-hack hard fork** story reinforces **fork-event awareness**
  (§22.4) — but it's historical background, not a trigger.
