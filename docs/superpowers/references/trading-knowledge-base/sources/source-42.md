> record it was seen and to reaffirm *why* options are excluded.

---

### 42.1 Options are excluded in their entirety → reaffirms §27.4 / §28.1 (no re-extraction)
An option is a **derivative** conferring a *right* (not ownership) on a future contract → excluded on three
grounds already established:
- **Gharar** — *"all financial derivative instruments … future agreements"* are excessive-uncertainty
  instruments (§28.1); options are the archetype.
- **Not spot / no ownership** — you hold a contract/right, not the underlying asset; our mandate is
  **spot long-only, you own the coin** (§28.2).
- **Maisir** — the fixed-premium, binary-ish payoff of buying an out-of-the-money option shades into a
  wager (§27.4, §28.1).

→ **Consistent with every prior options exclusion** in the KB (Deriv digital options §27.4; T3 options
sections §34.7; Adam Mesh options income §34.7). Nothing here changes or refines a rule — it's the same
excluded instrument, described at the intro level. **No `CompliancePolicy` change; no allowlist bearing**
(we trade spot crypto, not equity/crypto options).

### 42.2 Discarded (no agent value)
Call/put definitions, strike/expiration/premium mechanics, the SQN worked example, author bio, Swissquote
contact/marketing. All pertain to an instrument we do not and will not trade.
